A Scrivener project is a folder whose name ends in .scriv. The easiest way to bring it is as a zip: in Finder or Explorer, right-click the .scriv folder and compress it, then choose the .zip here. You can also choose the .scriv folder itself. Scrivener 2 and 3 projects are read.

{{if .Into}}Entities go into this project's primary Compendium if you may edit it. A name it already has stays a research note.{{else}}As entities, they go into a Compendium made for the new project. A sheet whose name is taken stays a research note.{{end}}

A picture is kept only if it is a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, and is cleaned first: where and with what it was taken, and any thumbnail, are removed.

The Draft becomes parts, chapters and scenes, with their titles, synopses, status and Include in Compile. Annotations and comments written in the text become private notes. PDFs and web pages in Research, labels, keywords, snapshots, collections and the Trash are counted, not brought in; the preview lists them.

In WordPress, open Tools, then Export, choose All content (or Posts) and download the export file. It is an .xml file. Choose it here, or a zip that holds it. To bring the pictures too, put the site's wp-content/uploads folder (from a backup, or your host's file manager) in the zip beside the export.

Posts become chapters of a blog project, oldest first, with the date and the excerpt. Comments are never read. Nothing is fetched from your site: a picture that is not in the zip is marked in the text with its description, and the preview lists the addresses.

A picture is kept only if it is a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, and is cleaned first: where and with what it was taken, and any thumbnail, are removed.

In Ghost Admin, open Settings, then Import/Export (under Advanced; Labs in older versions), and export your content. It downloads a .json file. Choose it here, or a zip that holds it. To bring the pictures too, put the site's content/images folder in the zip beside the export.

Posts become chapters of a blog project, oldest first, with the date and the excerpt. Members, newsletters and settings other than the site's title are not read. Nothing is fetched from your site: a picture that is not in the zip is marked in the text.

A picture is kept only if it is a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, and is cleaned first: where and with what it was taken, and any thumbnail, are removed.
