Compendium

{{.Project.Title}}. A Compendium holds the entries of a book: characters, places, items, organisations and ideas. A project sees its primary Compendium, the whole Compendiums it references, and any single entities linked to it. Being on this project does not put anyone on the Compendium: that is decided on the Compendium's own page.
