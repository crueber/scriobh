# Phase 7.5: the writing studio (goals, streaks, analytics, momentum)

Backend and ops only. **No screen is built here**: the dashboard, the goals panel, the sprint timer and the settings sections were built afterwards as the screens of [ui-studio.md](ui-studio.md) (FEATURES section BF), and the last section of this note says what each one needed. ## The rule that everything follows

**Goals count words added.** Net words are carried beside every figure and are never used to fail a day, a week or a goal. **A day is a goal day when a daily goal that was in force that day was met.** A day with no writing is empty: no value anywhere in this data is called missed, behind, failed or lost, and a test scans the catalogue's words for those. **All analytics are computed on this server** (F-514): no request leaves it, the only things that can leave are a card a person made (a link to this server's own page) and an email a person turned on.

## Decisions

**Almost no figure is stored person-level (the stored sums are below); everything is summed at read time from per-project rows.** `daily_stats` (user, project, day) is the rollup the save loop already keeps in its own transaction; the dashboard sums it over the projects the caller may count. A stored cross-project total could not drop a locked project afterwards. The list is `Viewer.IDs` (`data.go`): the person is still a member (rows of `daily_stats` and `goals` outlive a membership), the project is not trashed or purged, and the passphrase gate does not lock it for this caller (`projectgate.LockedProjects`, which treats a key that is not approved as locked). The progress card and the progress email have no session, so there the list drops every project that has a `project_gates` row at all (`NoSessionViewer`, and `visibleProjectsFor` in the mail service). A count of the projects left out (`hidden_projects`) is the only trace of them. **Days written in a project that is trashed, left or removed, or that the viewer cannot open, are not counted in their own streak and totals** until it counts again (a trash restored, a membership given back, a passphrase unlocked): a long streak can read 0 meanwhile. The rule is kept, and said where a streak is shown. **The rule for stored sums (fix round).** A goal version's `progress_at`, a total goal's `completed_on` and an archived goal's end are each derived from a sum written at some moment, from whatever the writer could count then. **A number shown to a viewer is judged only from the projects that viewer's figures include.** One function decides which (`Viewer`, `data.go`: `IDs` and `Partial`), and every consumer (the dashboard, series, calendar, compare, export, nudges, sprint statistics, the card and the email) builds its viewer through it: `newViewerOn` (a session or a key, passphrase gate asked), `NoSessionViewer` (the card: any project with a passphrase is out) and `ViewerFor` (the email: the list `visibleProjectsFor` gives). `Partial` is true when **any project the person has anything in is left out, for any reason**: locked, in the trash, not approved for a key, left or removed, or protected where there is no session. It is worked out from the memberships against the counted list, and from the projects that hold the person's daily rows (a skip scan, one index seek per project, so a project the person has left is seen too). When it is true, a goal over every project is judged by the version in force at the end of the period alone (`Goal.FinalOnly`: no `progress_at` shortcut), a stored completion of such a goal is not shown (the live figure decides), and an archived goal's end is worked out for that viewer; the streak says so (`streak.note`, `studio.streak.note`). A purged project cannot be seen (its rows go with it), and its words can only remain in a stored `progress_at` of a goal over every project; with the project gone this reveals nothing and is stated, not handled. The cost: while a project is left out, a past period of a raised goal may read as not met for the person, and it reads right again when it counts again. Tested by the matrix `TestAProjectLeftOutForAnyReasonNeverJudgesADay` (locked, trashed, left, unapproved key, no session; the dashboard, a warm public card and the email figures), `TestAGoalChangedBeforeALockDoesNotTellALockedReaderWhatWasWritten` and the review's `TestRvp75*` cases. Nothing is recomputed on the save path; `scriobh stats recompute` repairs `daily_stats` from `writing_sessions` and the dashboard then gives the same JSON byte for byte (tested on the whole dashboard, including after deleting every day row and rebuilding from the sessions).

**Goal versions and what judges a period** (`calc.go`, `Goal.judge`). A change adds a version (target, deadline, the local date it took effect, and the person's progress in that period at that moment). A period is met if, at some moment in it, the total reached the target then in force. That is what `daily_stats` already did for a day ("raising the goal later never takes a finished day away"), made the rule for weeks, months and custom goals. A version replaced inside the period is judged on the total at the moment it was replaced; the version in force at the end on the period's total. So lowering a goal helps the week it was lowered in, raising it never takes a met week away, and a week never judged against a target that did not exist yet. The displayed target of a past period is the version in force at its end. Archiving stores the date; the goal's last day is that date if its period was met by what the reader may count, else the day before (so what is met stays met). A goal that is archived cannot be changed; to go on, make a new one. Not built: un-archive (it would have to say what the gap days were).

**Nudge and target agree.** The nudge `met` and the "N words to go" message are both read from the same daily words goal (the headline), so one never says the goal is met while the other says words are to go.

**What each measure counts.** `words`: words added, from `daily_stats.words_added`. `sessions`: writing sessions begun that day. `minutes`: active writing time (the gaps between saves, each capped at five minutes), as `(seconds + 59) / 60` of the *sum* over projects, not a sum of rounded days. `chapters`: **a document of kind `chapter` set to `done` for the first time by that person**, recorded in `studio_chapters_done` by `doc.update` in the same transaction (a fault in it is logged and never fails the save); nothing recorded chapter completion per day before, so chapters finished before 1.2 are not counted, a chapter finished twice counts once, a note or scene marked done does not count, and a trashed chapter no longer counts. A **total** goal in words is measured against the **words in the manuscript** (the live documents of the project, or of every project the caller may count: the figure `project.get` reports), because "reach 80,000 words" is about the size of the book; every other measure is counted from the goal's start date. This is the one place a goal reads a net figure: its value. Its forecast is read from the net words, as it was (a fix round briefly read it from the words added and was reversed: a word-count target is reached by the manuscript growing, so a pace of added words would project a finish the manuscript will not reach). `forecast.basis.measure` is `net` for words and `added` for every other measure, `forecast.unit` names the measure, and the rate is worded "net words a day". **Completion is sticky**: a total goal that has been reached stays completed, with `completed_on` (the day a read first saw it reached), however much is cut afterwards, and `goals_completed` on the card does not fall; only the person raising the target makes it active again (lowering it below the words completes it again). It is stored by the first owner read (`studio.dashboard` or `studio.goal.list`, once per goal, the only write a read makes, so a read-scope key's read can make it; the card and the email never write) and, for a goal over every project, only by a read that counts every project the person has written in, and it is not shown to a viewer who cannot (rule above: the live figure decides). The goal set on a project's Goals page (Phase 6, `stats.project`) is left as it was: it is judged on the live manuscript and can fall back to active after a cut.

**The forecast** (`calc.go`, `forecast`). Window: the 28 days ending today. `per_day` is the window's total over 28 (writing days or not). `projected_date` is today plus `ceil(remaining * 28 / total)` days in integer arithmetic (300 to go at 300 in 28 days is exactly 28), given only when the window has at least five writing days and growth, and not beyond ten years; absent is the honest answer. `required_per_day` is `ceil(remaining / days_left)` counting today. `margin_days` is the deadline minus the projected date: information, never a verdict. Every forecast says `estimate: true` with its basis (window, writing days, per-day pace, net words) and as message keys (`notes`); a projection later than the deadline is not stated in days (`studio.forecast.behind` is no longer answered).

**Streaks.** A day is a goal day when any daily goal in force that day was met by the versions then in force: a studio daily goal (any measure, any scope) or a goal set on a project's Goals page (`goal.set`, whose met days `daily_stats.goal_hit` already freezes). `goals.Streaks` counts the run, so today does not break it until it is over, and dates are stepped as calendar dates, so a 23- or 25-hour day neither skips nor repeats a date. A person who has no daily goal sees `has_goal: false` and `studio.streak.set_goal`. `today.target` and "N words to go today" read the daily words goal over every project if there is one, else the one nearest to being met.

**Time.** A day is the person's local date in their profile zone when the save was made, stored per session and per day, so changing zone never re-buckets history (tested: Auckland to Los Angeles across the clocks going back: the 6th holds the 80 written in Auckland and the 20 written in Los Angeles). The injected clock is `db.SetClock`; the pure functions take "today" as an argument.

**The calendar's five steps.** Step 0 is an empty day. Steps 1 to 4 are cut at the 25th, 50th and 75th percentile (nearest rank) of the person's **own non-zero days, all of their history** (not the window shown, so a day keeps its colour as the window moves): a day above the third cut is 4, above the second 3, above the first 2, otherwise 1. If every writing day has the same words, they are all step 4. The cuts, their count and the `uniform` flag are in the answer for a legend. Weeks run Monday to Sunday, 24 columns ending with today's week; days after today are `future`; the month label is on the column that holds the first of the month (the first column is labelled with its own month); `label_rows` is `[0, 2, 4]` for Monday, Wednesday and Friday. `hit` marks a goal day; nothing marks any other day.

**Patterns.** The best weekday is read from the days; the hour is the local hour **a writing session began in**, from `writing_sessions.started_at` and `tz` (so a recompute and the dashboard agree), and a session's words are not split across hours (stated wherever it is shown).

**Sprints** (`sprint.go`, migration 1931). A sprint is a window on one project (15, 25, 45 or 1 to 240 minutes) or an open session timer (0, at most four hours). The words in it are the save loop's own additions: `goals.record` adds each save's credit to the day and, in the same transaction and one indexed probe, to the running sprint of that person on that project, so each word is counted once in the day it was written on and once in the sprint it was written during, and never twice in any day (tested: a sprint that holds everything of its day equals the day; one holding a part is smaller; saves on another project or after the closing time are in the day and not in the sprint). **A sprint's words are the words recorded while it ran, not a subset of one day**: a sprint that runs past local midnight (or across a change of zone) holds the words of two days, so it reconciles with the sum of the days it touches over the time it ran (`TestRvp75ASprintAcrossMidnightIsLargerThanItsDay`: 30 words before midnight and 70 after make a sprint of 100 beside days of 30 and 70), and it counts on the day it began for the best sprint and the time-of-day figures. One at a time per person (a partial unique index; a second start is 409). **A sprint left running is closed by a rule of its stored times**: saves count until `closes_at` (the planned end and two minutes of grace to finish a sentence, or four hours for an open timer), a read treats the row as completed from then on (ended at its planned end), nothing is written by a read and there is no timer, the next sprint write settles the row, and a restore closes every sprint (`backup.PostRestore`). It is called completed, never failed. `abandon` keeps the words in the day and leaves the sprint out of the statistics, saying nothing about it. Sprint statistics count completed and stopped sprints: how many, words, words per sprint, the best one (the earlier wins a tie), and the quarter of the day they began in.

**The open timer** counts as the four hours it can run (240 minutes in the sprint statistics) when nobody stops it; stop it, and it counts what it ran. **A restore** closes a running sprint at its planned end (or the four-hour cap), never later than the last save credited to the person on that project since it began and never later than now, so a 25-minute sprint restored ten days later is still at most 25 minutes.

**Nudges.** The server returns keys and parameters, never English. The candidates are a pure function of today's numbers: `studio.nudge.met`, `.streak` (3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365, 500, 1000 days), `.half` (50% of today's goal), `.keep_streak`, `.to_go`. At most three are offered a day; a nudge shown is recorded with `studio.nudge.seen` and is not offered again that day; with the preference `studio.nudges` off the dashboard offers none. The read is pure: it writes nothing. The trend compares the last 21 days with the 21 before (`up` and `down` at 10% or more, `steady`, `new` when there was nothing before, `none`) and offers three resources (`sprint`, `goal`, `calendar`) only when it is `down`; nothing in it is a warning, and a test refuses the words of shame in the catalogue.

**The export.** CSV tables `daily`, `weekly`, `goals`, `sprints`, `projects`, and one Markdown file. Deterministic (nothing in a file is the time it was made), the caller's own data over the projects they may count, a range of at most ten years, a 2 MB bound that the ranges stay far under, and a file name made from the table and the dates only. **CSV and a negative number:** a text cell that begins with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return gets a leading apostrophe; a number is written as a number and is not touched, so a net figure of `-100` stays a number (it cannot be a formula, and making it text would break every column that sums). A day on which a goal was met has `1` in `goal_met` and every other day has nothing there.

**The private card.** Table `studio_cards`: the token is 32 random bytes with an id (`crd_`), only its SHA-256 is stored, constant-time compare, one `404` page for every token that opens nothing (the share links' "gone" page), expiry 1 to 365 days (30 default), revocable, at most five live, a view count and last view time for the owner (the page counts a view; the SVG and the JSON do not). It shows the streak and the longest, writing days in the last 30, the active and completed goal counts and up to three goals as numbers and the words for their period and measure; **no project title unless that card was made with `show_projects`, never a document title, comment or word of text, never a name, never the owner's label**. It is made at every request from `NoSessionViewer`, so a project that gets a passphrase, goes to the trash or is left is off every card at once. Public handling is the share links': `isSharePath` now covers `/card/` (no session or key is read, no `Set-Cookie`, the token is not in the log, `noindex, nofollow, noarchive`, `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`, a 32 KB body limit), and one rate limit per client address before the token is looked at (`SCRIOBH_SHARE_RATE_LIMIT`). The SVG is drawn on the server with flat fills, a hairline and presentation attributes only (no gradient, `<style>`, script, image, link or font file) with every word escaped; the PNG is made by the screen from the SVG in a canvas, and no rasteriser was added. I did not reuse `share_links` or its token regular expression: that table has a project foreign key and its tokens are `shl_`. **What a request costs** (fix round): a public GET never writes. Views are counted in memory per token and written in one transaction at most once a minute and when the server stops (`Cards.Flush`; a failed write puts the counts back), and `studio.card.list` adds the ones not yet written. What a card shows is worked out at most once a minute for each card (the numbers only), at most four cards at a time, and requests that arrive together share one computation. The token is read live on every request with one indexed read, so a revoked or expired link, or a disabled owner, is the one 404 at once however warm the cache is; a fingerprint read on every request (the passphrases that exist and the newest change, and the person's projects: those that count and all of them) drops the cache at once when a passphrase is set, changed or cleared or a project is trashed, restored, left or joined. Numbers written inside the minute (a save, a new goal) show at the next minute. The per-address limit is the share links' (`SCRIOBH_SHARE_RATE_LIMIT`, **120 requests a minute by default**, an IPv6 /64 counting as one address, burst 10), and the four-at-a-time cap bounds what many addresses together can make the server compute. Revoked and expired cards older than 90 days are deleted by an hourly sweep (at most 500 a pass, the count logged). The text of a card is made plain once, where the view is built (`cleanText`: what XML 1.0 forbids, bidirectional and invisible characters and invalid UTF-8 dropped; a project's title at most 40 characters, a goal's line 52, cut with an ellipsis before escaping), so the page, the picture and the data agree and the SVG is always well-formed (`TestTheCardSVGIsAlwaysWellFormed`, `FuzzCardSVG`).

**The progress email** (`internal/mail/progress.go`, migration 1934). Opt-in, off by default, weekly (Monday 07:00 local plus up to 30 minutes from the person's id, for the ISO week before) or monthly (the first, for the month before), bound to the account address it was turned on for (a change of address stops it, `needs_confirm`), once per period (the schedule row and the queued message in one transaction, a dedupe key `progress:<person>:<period>`), not for a period that has nothing to say (words, minutes and sessions all zero over the projects the person may count: the period is still marked done), not for a person unseen for 90 days. The first message is for the first whole period after turning it on. The body is fixed words and numbers; a test matches every line against the allowed shapes. It goes through `visibleProjectsFor`, so a protected project, a trashed one or one the person left contributes nothing, and **the message is made again from its dates at the moment it is sent** (a project locked or trashed while the relay was down is not counted; a message with nothing left to say is cancelled). `mail_outbox.kind` has a CHECK list, so migration 1934 rebuilds the table with `progress` (rows and indexes carried over, tested). `List-Unsubscribe` is the app's own settings page, as for the digest; **the settings page must carry the switch** (the unsubscribe address points there). A restore pauses it with the rest of mail (`PauseAll` empties the queue and switches mail off), `auth.LostAccess` deletes the opt-in and cancels what waits, and switching mail on again starts everyone after that moment, so nothing written while it was off is sent.

**The index.** Migration 1935 adds a covering index on `daily_stats` for the dashboard's read (see Measured).

## What a screen needs

**Offline.** Every page is `no-store` and the service worker keeps no page, so none of this is cached. A screen shows its last answer from memory and says that it is out of date; it never writes a number itself. The only writes are the ones below, each of which is safe to repeat with an `Idempotency-Key`; a screen offline holds a nudge's id and sends `studio.nudge.seen` when it can. The sprint timer runs from `started_at`, `ends_at` and `remaining_seconds` in the client's own clock and corrects itself from `studio.sprint.current`; nothing depends on the page staying open (the rule closes the sprint at the server).

**The dashboard page** (`GET /api/v1/me/studio`; a page route is the UI agent's). Every number and key:

- `day`, `timezone` (change it in the profile), `hidden_projects` (a count; say "N projects are locked and not counted", nothing more).
- `today`: `words`, `net`, `minutes`, `sessions`, `has_goal`, `goal_id`, `target`, `remaining`, `percent`, `hit`, `message` (`studio.today.to_go` with `n`, `studio.today.met`). Counts take `.one` and `.other` forms: use `N(key, params.n)`.
- `streak` (the habit card): `has_goal`, `current`, `longest`, `today_words`, `today_target`, `today_hit`, `message` (`studio.streak.hit` and `.keep` with `days` and `n` count forms, `.begin`, `.set_goal`). No state for a day gone by: do not draw one (the fix round extended this to the numeral of a run of 0). `measure`, `today_value` and `today_target` are the daily goal the card reads, in its measure; `today` words are that goal's own when it belongs to one project (`today.project_id`).
- `goals`: `active`, `completed`, `archived` ("2 active, 1 completed"), `items[]` with `id`, `source` (`studio` or `project`; a `project` goal is changed on the project's Goals page and its id starts `project:`), `project_id`, `project_title`, `project_slug`, `period`, `measure`, `target`, `value`, `remaining`, `percent` (may exceed 100), `met`, `status`, `period_from`, `period_to`, `start_date`, `deadline`, `version`, `changes`, `net` (words goals: show it beside, never against), `forecast` (total goals), `message` (`studio.goal.met` or `studio.goal.remaining`, with `period`, `measure`, `n`; the words for them are `studio.period.*` and `studio.measure.*`).
- `forecast`: `estimate` (always true: say "estimate"), `remaining`, `deadline`, `days_left`, `required_per_day`, `projected_date`, `margin_days`, `basis` (`window_days`, `writing_days`, `per_day`, `measure`) and `notes[]` as keys: `studio.forecast.required`, `.projected`, `.none`, `.ahead`, `.behind` (count forms), `.basis`. Show the basis next to the date.
- `cards[]`: four, `key` `today`, `7d`, `30d`, `365d`, `days`, `from`, `to`, `words`, `net`, `minutes`, `sessions`, `writing_days`, `previous` (the same figures), `delta` (`change`, `percent` or null), `delta_minutes`, `sparkline[]` (7 days for today, daily for 7d and 30d, 52 weeks for 365d). Percent null means "nothing before": show no percentage.
- `series`: `range`, `from`, `to`, `bucket` (`day`, `week`, `month`), `points[]` (`start`, `words`, `net`, `minutes`), `figures`, `avg_per_day`, `avg_per_writing_day`, `weekdays[]` (Monday first: `words`, `writing_days`, `avg_per_writing_day`), `best_weekday`, `hours[]` (24), `parts[]` (`night`, `morning`, `afternoon`, `evening`: `studio.part.*`), `best_hour` (or null). The range selector is `range` (`30d`, `90d`, `365d`, `all`) or `from` and `to`; `GET /api/v1/me/studio/series` takes the same.
- `calendar`: `from`, `to`, `weeks[]` (`start` Monday, `month` 1 to 12 on the column that holds a month's first day: name it with `studio.month_short.N`, `cells[]` seven days Monday first: `day`, `words`, `level` 0 to 4, `hit`, `future`), `label_rows` (`[0,2,4]`: label those rows with `studio.weekday_short.N`), `levels` (5), `thresholds` (`cuts`, `uniform`, `days`: a legend: "darker means more of your own days"), `writing_days`, `words`, `all_time_writing_days`. "All time": `GET /api/v1/me/studio/calendar?all=true` (or `weeks=N` up to 530). Colour 0 is the page's own surface, not a red or a grey X; do not mark an empty day.
- `projects[]`: `project_id`, `title`, `slug`, `today`, `week`, `previous_week`, `week_net`: the person's own words only.
- `trend`: `recent_words`, `previous_words`, `change_percent`, `direction`, `message_key` (`studio.trend.<direction>`), `resources[]` (`key`, `action` `sprint`, `goal` or `calendar`, `params`): offered, never required, never styled as an alert.
- `nudges[]`: `id`, `key`, `params`, `kind`; show at most what is returned, then post their ids to `POST /api/v1/me/studio/nudges/seen` (`{"ids": [...]}`; answers `seen_today`, `left`). `nudges_enabled` is the preference `studio.nudges` (`prefs.set`, `"true"` or `"false"`): the switch lives in settings.
- `sprint` (the running one) and `resume` (`project_id`, `slug`, `title`: the project last written in).

**"Write N words today"** (the quick action; F-501). `today.remaining` is N. The button opens `resume.slug`'s writing view and starts a session with `POST /api/v1/projects/{resume.project_id}/studio/sprints` and `{"minutes": 0, "target_words": N}` (an open timer with a target; or `{"minutes": 25}` for a sprint). The writing view then shows `studio.sprint.current` (`remaining_seconds`, `words_added` of `target_words`) and the existing "Today N of M" figure; the words are counted by the saves the editor already makes, so the page does nothing to count them. `studio.sprint.stop` ends it; `abandon` sets it aside. `409 sprint_running` carries the reason when one is already running: offer to stop it.

**Goals screen.** `studio.goal.list?archived=true`, `studio.goal.create` (`period`, `measure`, `target`, `project_id` empty for every project, `deadline` for a total goal), `studio.goal.update` (`target`, `deadline`), `studio.goal.archive`, `studio.goal.history?periods=12` (`versions[]`, `periods[]` each judged against the target of its time, `net`). Errors a screen handles: 422 with the field and a plain sentence; 409 `goal_exists` with `goal_id`; 409 `too_many_goals`; 409 archived; 404 for a goal that is not the person's or whose project is locked or in the trash. A period that was not met is "not met" in a quiet style, once, in the history; never in the dashboard.

**Sprint statistics.** `studio.sprint.stats?range=`: `sprints`, `words`, `avg_per_sprint`, `minutes`, `best` (`words_added`, `day`, `minutes`), `parts[]` (by time of day), `recent[]`.

**Export.** `GET /api/v1/me/studio/export?format=csv|md&table=daily|weekly|goals|sprints|projects&range=` is a download (link to it, with the cookie).

**Card.** In settings: `studio.card.create` (`label`, `show_projects`, `expires_days`) shows the address once (`path`, `/card/<token>`; build the full link from the page's own origin); `studio.card.list` shows each with `views`, `expires_at`, `revoked`, `expired`; `studio.card.revoke`. PNG: fetch `/card/<token>/card.svg`, draw it on a canvas and save the canvas (no server rasteriser); `/card/<token>/card.json` has the same numbers. The page, the SVG and the JSON are served; their words come from `studio.card.*` in the reader's language (`Accept-Language`). Say plainly: anyone with the link sees the streak and goals, not a word of any project.

**Progress email section** (settings, beside the digest): `mail.progress.get` (`mail_available`, `enabled`, `frequency`, `needs_confirm`, `email`, `contents`) and `mail.progress.set` (`enabled`, `frequency` `weekly` or `monthly`); 409 `mail_off` says mail is not switched on. Words: `studio.mail.*`.

**Where the keys are.** `internal/i18n/locales/en.json`, prefix `studio.`: `today`, `goal`, `streak`, `nudge`, `trend`, `resource`, `forecast`, `card`, `sprint`, `mail`, and the vocabulary `weekday`, `weekday_short`, `month`, `month_short`, `period`, `measure`, `part`. A key with a count has `.one` and `.other` forms (`TestEveryMessageKeyTheStudioCanReturnIsInTheCatalogue` checks every key the server writes). The `studio.sprint.*` and `studio.mail.*` words are provided for the screens and used by no template yet.

## Limits

| What | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Active goals | 20 a person; one per scope, period and measure |
| A range | ten years (3,660 days); calendar 530 weeks; goal history 200 periods |
| A sprint | 1 to 240 minutes, or an open timer of at most 4 hours; two minutes of grace |
| Nudges | 3 a day, none twice, 10 ids per call |
| Cards | 5 live; 1 to 365 days; a label of 60 characters |
| Export | at most ten years of days, 2 MB |
| Progress email | weekly or monthly; 3 a person an hour like the digest; 6 hours old at most when sent |

## What was not verified

- **No screen, no browser.** Nothing here was seen in a browser; the card page was rendered through the server's handler chain and read as text, and the SVG was checked as text (no gradient, script, link or font), not looked at. How the SVG draws, and the canvas PNG, are untested. Contrast, 390 px, keyboard use and the offline reload belong to the screens.
- **The full bench** (`make bench`) and the e2e scripts were not run, by instruction. The `studio.dashboard` metric ran only inside `TestRunSmoke` (3 runs); **`bench/baseline.json` was not touched**: it needs a `make bench --write-baseline` on a quiet machine, and the generator and its seed were left alone so the other metrics stay comparable.
- The ledger's counting rule for words added is the existing one (`words.go`); its approximations (a replacement by the same word earns nothing, a move between documents is credited again) apply to every figure here and were not changed.
- The email was tested against the fake relay of `internal/mail`; a real relay, a real mailbox and how a client shows the plain text were not seen. A daylight-saving change at 07:00 on a Monday was not tested for the email (the periods and slots are pure functions tested with fixed dates and zones).
- The dashboard's performance on a database with far more than five years or 20 projects was not measured; the read is linear in the person's daily rows.
- A project's own totals for other members (what a team did) are **not** shown anywhere: the rule that other members' numbers are never visible was kept.
- `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`'s budget table was not edited (it is not in the files this work owns); the budget is stated here, in OPERATIONS and in `internal/bench/run.go`, and the coordinator may want a row.
- The generated tables of `docs/API.md`, `internal/cli/snapshot.json`, `skills/` and `internal/mcp/tools.snapshot.json` are stale until `make skill` runs (the two catalogue snapshot tests fail until then, by design).

## Not built, by decision

Un-archiving a goal; a goal over a team or a project's other members; weekly and monthly goals measured on net words; social posting of any kind; a server-side PNG; push notifications; the progress email as HTML or with a tracking pixel; an email for a person's goal reached (nudges are in-app only); comparing two people; and "view all time" for the period cards (they are fixed periods; the series and the calendar have the all-time views).
